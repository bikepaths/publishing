Sincerity as a Signaling Problem

Watzlawick's axiom "one cannot not communicate" is the standard reference point in communication theory: within a relational context, the absence of a response is itself interpreted as data by the receiver, regardless of the sender's intent. Silence is underdetermined, it's compatible with many internal states (avoidance, self-protection, indifference, overwhelm), which means the interpreter fills the gap using their own priors, not the silent party's actual state.
